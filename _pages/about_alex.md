[Alex Warstadt](https://alexwarstadt.github.io/) is an Assistant Professor in Linguistics and he Halicioglu Data Science Institute at UC San Diego. His research focuses on interdisciplinary research in linguistics, computational cognitive modeling, and natural language processing. He uses advances in machine learning to understand why human language is the way it is, how children come to acquire it, and how information is conveyed across multiple channels. His lab use insights from linguistics and cognitive science to advance compute- and data-efficient learning in LMs and to evaluate and interpret how LMs learn and represent grammatical structures and meaning.
